## 🙋 About Me

I'm a backend-focused developer based in **Poland**, currently studying at **Politechnika Częstochowska**. My passion is building robust, scalable backend systems — from well-structured monoliths to distributed microservice architectures. I enjoy working close to the metal of the JVM while staying pragmatic about when to reach for reactive, event-driven, or stream-processing tools.

When I'm not diving into Java, I complement my backend work with frontend skills to bring full ideas to life end-to-end.
